Frequently asked questions

Is the price per person?

No. Prices are per vehicle — a fixed rate regardless of traffic or time of day. Maximum 4 people per service.

Do you also go to the cruise terminal?

Yes: cruise terminal to Aeroparque (USD 35) and to Ezeiza (USD 70).

Is it really private?

Yes. Only your group travels — no strangers, no third-party schedules. Pace, stops and route are designed around you. Maximum 4 people per service.

How do I pay?

Prices are in US dollars, cash. Credit card payments carry a 12% surcharge.

Do I have to pay to ask for a date?

No. Send the form or a WhatsApp message and Diego replies with availability. No payment, no commitment.

Which language is the tour in?

English, French or Spanish — Diego speaks all three.
